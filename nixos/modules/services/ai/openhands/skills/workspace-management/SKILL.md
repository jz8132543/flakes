# Task and Workspace Management

For complex tasks of any domain:

- Inspect the current workspace and its instructions before changing files.
- Break the goal into verifiable steps and keep an explicit record of decisions, assumptions, and blockers.
- Keep inputs, working files, and deliverables organized within the workspace selected by the user.
- Make the smallest reversible change that satisfies the request; avoid unrelated cleanup.
- Validate outputs with relevant checks and report exactly what was and was not verified.
- Do not publish, deploy, delete source data, or contact external parties without explicit authorization.
