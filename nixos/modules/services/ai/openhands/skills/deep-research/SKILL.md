# Deep Research and Source Tracking

For research tasks of any subject:

- Convert the request into answerable questions and identify what evidence would resolve each one.
- Prefer primary sources and independent corroboration. Record source titles, URLs, publication dates, and access dates.
- Use search results to discover sources, then read the underlying pages or documents before relying on them.
- Separate sourced facts, reasonable inference, uncertainty, and recommendation.
- Preserve quotations only when needed, keep them brief, and attribute them precisely.
- Save reusable notes and source metadata in the active workspace; do not silently mix unrelated projects.
- State gaps and conflicting evidence rather than filling them with invented details.
